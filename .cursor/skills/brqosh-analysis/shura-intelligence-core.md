## 3. Shura session — 8 rounds (v3 Supreme)

| # | Round | Duration | Rule |
|---|-------|----------|------|
| **1** | **facts** | numbers only | no opinion · no "seems" · 4 closure levels |
| **2** | **initial positions** | ≤3 sentences/seat | S01 positive · S02 negative · rest specialty |
| **3** | **debate** | disagreement only | every objection needs ID from CEEF or CSV |
| **3b** | **adversarial verify** | critical claims | 3 votes: S02+S03+S12 — 2/3 refute = kill claim |
| **4** | **conditional vote** | ✅/🟡/❌ | ≥9/12 approve/conditional · ≤1 oppose on P0 |
| **5** | **conflict resolution** | domain priority | security > accounting > verify > architecture > product > UX |
| **6** | **facilitator synthesis** | sentence + steelman | summarizes · saves dissent in MEM |

| **8** | **leak synthesis + supreme gate** | integrity score | 10 families · 6 gates · certify |
